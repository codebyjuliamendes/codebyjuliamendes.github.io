Secretária IA

A multi-tenant virtual receptionist on WhatsApp. It answers patients, books only into real free slots and hands over to a human when needed. Built first for health and aesthetics clinics, now for any appointment-based business.

Team

Being offered to early customers

01 — Problem

Small businesses lose bookings in the inbox.

Clinics and service businesses answer clients on WhatsApp by hand, often outside working hours. Messages wait, slots go unfilled and the team is interrupted all day to confirm times.

The goal: an assistant that holds the conversation, checks the real agenda, books or cancels, and never pretends to know what it does not, while each business keeps its own data fully separate.

03 — Decisions & trade-offs

Why it was built this way.

Book only into free slots

The model proposes, the backend verifies. Appointments are created only in slots that are actually available, so the AI cannot double-book.

Tenant isolation from the URL

Each clinic is isolated by design, with role-based permissions (owner, manager, staff, super admin) and audit logs.

Persistent queue instead of fire-and-forget

Outgoing messages live in Postgres and are retried with backoff, so a provider hiccup does not lose a reply.

Security on every edge

Short-lived JWTs with rotating refresh tokens kept in httpOnly cookies through a backend-for-frontend, signed webhooks and rate limiting.

Built for the business side too

Plans with quotas, Stripe billing, Pix and boleto, Google Calendar sync, a human-handoff inbox, return campaigns and LGPD data-deletion support.

Measure conversation quality

Observability per tenant

Dashboards for latency, LLM cost per conversation and queue health to price plans on real data.
